Shipping & Returns

How your tea gets to you, and what happens if something is wrong with it.

Dispatch & timelines

  • Same-day dispatch before 2pmOrders placed after 2pm, or on Sundays, go out the next working morning.
  • Bangalore: 1–2 working daysOften the same evening for Indiranagar and nearby areas.
  • Rest of India: 3–6 working daysNorth-east, J&K and island territories may take 7–10 days.
  • Tracking by SMS and emailSent the moment your parcel leaves Bangalore.

We currently ship within India only. For international orders, write to us and we will quote a courier rate.

Returns & refunds

Tea is a food product, so we cannot accept a return simply because a blend was not to your taste — though do tell us, because we would rather recommend a better one.

We will replace or refund in full if:

  • The packet arrives damaged, open or wet.
  • You received a different blend or pack size from the one you ordered.
  • The product is past, or close to, its best-before date on arrival.
  • Your order never arrived and tracking shows no movement for 10 days.

Tell us within 7 days of delivery — call 00000 00000 or email hello@teastall-demo.com with your order reference and a photo. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are dispatched — usually a window of a few hours. Once a parcel has left Bangalore it cannot be cancelled, but the returns terms above still apply.
