Our Story

One kettle, one recipe, one very long queue. Not much has changed since 2007 — and that is rather the point.

How it started

A corner, a kettle, and one blend

In 2007 we set a kettle down at #00, Demo Street, Indiranagar, with one blend and no plan at all. The idea was simple and a little unfashionable — grind our own spices every week, refuse premix, and never cut the leaf with anything cheaper.

Eighteen years on, the recipe has not moved a gram. Seven branches have opened, eight hundred gatherings have been poured for, and six thousand people a day still queue for a cup that costs less than a bus fare.

We began sealing packets in 2021, because the people who had left Bangalore would not stop asking. Everything on this site is the tea we are pouring at the stall this morning.

Our rules

Four shortcuts we never take

Most of what makes the tea good is a list of things we have refused to do.

🚫

No premix

Every spice arrives whole and is ground here. Premix is cheaper, faster, and tastes like it.

🌿

No essence

If the packet says rose, there are rose petals in it. Flavouring syrup has never come through the door.

📅

No old stock

We grind to the week ahead. A blend showing out of stock is a blend being made, not a blend being hoarded.

💚

No secret price

The packet costs what the stall charges, plus shipping. There is no online markup.
