Why Sharon Tea

Nothing hurried, nothing hidden

We are a stall before we are a brand. What we seal into a packet is what we pour into a cup — same road, same morning.

Ethically sourced

Single-origin leaves from certified estates in Assam, the Nilgiris and Idukki. No colour, no essence.

Fresh-brewed, never pre-mixed

Whole spices ground in small weekly batches. The oils are still alive when the packet is sealed.

Sealed the same day

Triple-layer pouches with a one-way valve. The aroma stays in and the monsoon stays out.

Sent across India

Out of Bangalore in a day, tracked all the way to your door. Free above ₹999.

Our story

It started with one kettle

In 2007 there was a kettle, a corner of Bangalore, and a single blend. The recipe has not moved since. Only the queue has — six thousand of you now, every day.

  • Weighed by handThe same two people have measured every spice ratio since the first morning.
  • Nothing waits in a warehouseWe grind to the week ahead. A blend that is out of stock is a blend being made.
  • The stall is still openCome and taste before you buy a packet. We would rather you did.
