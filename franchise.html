Why franchise with us

A small footprint with a very long queue

A tea stall needs less space, less staff and less capital than almost any other food business — and it sells every single day of the year.

📐

120–250 sq ft is enough

A corner unit, a kiosk in an office lobby, a stall in a market. No kitchen licence for the standard format.

📦

Blends supplied weekly

You never blend anything. Sealed, ground-that-week packets arrive from Bangalore on a fixed schedule.

🎓

Two weeks of training

Your team learns at the Indiranagar stall — brewing, portioning, billing, hygiene, and the rhythm of a queue.

🎨

Branding done for you

Signage, uniforms, cups, menu boards and social templates. You do not hire a designer.

📊

Billing & stock system

A simple POS with daily sales and stock reporting, so reordering is one tap and not a guess.

🤝

Territory protection

No second Sharon stall within 2 km of yours. Your catchment stays yours.

The process

From enquiry to first cup in about ten weeks

01

Enquiry & call

You send the form below. We call within two working days to understand your city and budget.

02

Site approval

You shortlist locations; we visit or review footfall data before signing off on one.

03

Agreement & fit-out

Five-year agreement, then four to six weeks of fit-out using our drawings and vendors.

04

Training & launch

Two weeks of training in Bangalore, then we run your first three days on site with you.
